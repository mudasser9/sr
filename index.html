02 // ABOUT

About

I work the queue — alert triage, log correlation, and closing the loop between "something fired" and "here's what happened and why." My focus is Tier 1/2 SOC operations: reducing false positives, writing detections that hold up under real traffic, and documenting incidents so the next analyst doesn't start from zero.

I'm most interested in the handoff points other analysts skip — turning a one-off investigation into a reusable detection rule, and turning a messy incident into a playbook someone can follow at 3am.

  • SIEMSplunk, Microsoft Sentinel, Elastic (ELK)
